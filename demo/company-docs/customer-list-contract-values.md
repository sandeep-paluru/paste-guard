CONFIDENTIAL. Prepared by Finance and Customer Success for the quarterly business review. Contains customer names, contract values and renewal risk ratings. Restricted to the executive team and account directors.

Summary. Calloway ended Q3 2026 with 1,284 paying customers and 61.7 million dollars of annual recurring revenue. The top 25 accounts represent 44 percent of ARR. Gross revenue retention for the quarter was 91.2 percent and net revenue retention was 112 percent.

- Harborline Logistics: 2.84 million dollars ACV, renews March 2027, risk LOW, expansion opportunity in APAC
- Cedarpoint Health Network: 2.31 million dollars ACV, renews January 2027, risk HIGH, champion left in August
- Bramblewood Retail Group: 1.97 million dollars ACV, renews June 2027, risk MEDIUM, evaluating Quillmark
- Ostrander Mutual Insurance: 1.66 million dollars ACV, renews November 2026, risk LOW
- Kestrel Aerospace Components: 1.42 million dollars ACV, renews April 2027, risk MEDIUM, budget freeze
- Velmont University System: 1.18 million dollars ACV, renews July 2027, risk LOW
- Tallis Freight Partners: 0.96 million dollars ACV, renews December 2026, risk HIGH, payment 60 days late

At risk accounts. Cedarpoint Health Network is the single largest renewal risk in the next two quarters. Their VP of Data, our executive sponsor, departed in August and the replacement has a history with Brightlayer. We have offered a 14 percent discount on a three year renewal and a free streaming pilot. If Cedarpoint churns, Q1 2027 net new ARR will be negative.

Tallis Freight Partners has an outstanding receivable of 238,000 dollars. Collections has escalated twice. Recommendation is to suspend non production workspaces on November 15 if payment is not received, while protecting the production tenant to avoid breach of the SLA.

Expansion pipeline. Harborline Logistics is piloting the AI query assistant with 400 users and has verbally committed to a 600,000 dollar expansion in Q1. Velmont University is negotiating a system wide agreement covering four additional campuses which could add 750,000 dollars ACV at education pricing.

Concentration risk. No single customer exceeds 5 percent of ARR, which remains within the covenant in our credit facility. However, the top three logistics customers together represent 9.8 percent, and the board has asked for a sector concentration review.

Methodology notes. ACV figures reflect contracted recurring fees and exclude professional services and one time implementation fees. Risk ratings are assigned by the account director and reviewed by the VP of Customer Success each month using health score, executive engagement, product usage trends, support ticket volume and payment history. Accounts flagged HIGH for two consecutive months are escalated to the executive sponsor program, which pairs each account with a member of the leadership team who meets the customer at least monthly until the rating improves.
