CONFIDENTIAL. Notes taken by Lena Brandt (GC, board secretary). Draft minutes, not yet approved by the board. Attendees: Dana Whitcombe (CEO), Raj Okafor (CFO), directors E. Saarinen (chair), M. Abiodun, C. Lyle, T. Fairbanks; observers from two funds present for open session only.

## Deck outline as presented

1. CEO overview (Dana, 15 min). 2. Financials and forecast (Raj, 20 min). 3. Ferrolake acquisition (Dana + Raj, 30 min, closed). 4. Security incident update (Kenji Torres joined, 15 min). 5. Rennick litigation (Lena, 10 min, closed). 6. Q4 restructuring, Project Juniper (closed, CEO/CFO only, 20 min). 7. Comp committee report (C. Lyle, 10 min).

## 1. CEO overview

Dana opened with Q3: ARR 61.7M vs plan 62.1M, the miss is two deals (Northfork Regional Bank, Granger-Holt expansion) that slipped into October, both now verbally committed. Gross margin 76%. Burn down to 1.9M a month. Saarinen asked whether the slip pattern is a sales execution issue; Dana said partly, the new procurement step requiring security questionnaires added about 3 weeks on average since the incident.

Raj walked through the 2027 plan of 92M ending ARR. Abiodun pushed back that 49% growth is aggressive given Q3; Raj said the plan assumes 6.5M of ARR from Riptrace streaming and 3.1M from Lantern, and without Ferrolake the plan would be closer to 82M. Board asked for a downside case without Ferrolake for the December meeting. ACTION: Raj.

## 3. Ferrolake

Discussion of the memo circulated Sept 14. Fairbanks asked why not just partner with Ferrolake. Dana: partnership means they remain acquirable by Varnetta, and their runway is 7 months. Lyle raised concern about paying 6.7x ARR for a company with 7 months of cash; Raj noted the earnout and that effective upfront multiple is closer to 5.7x. Saarinen asked about the GPL issue; Lena confirmed it will be a pre close covenant.

RESOLVED: the board authorizes the CEO to sign a non binding LOI with Ferrolake Data Systems at an enterprise value not to exceed 41.5M, substantially on the terms in the memo. Vote: 5 in favor, 1 abstain (Fairbanks, citing a potential conflict through a fund LP relationship).

## 4. Security incident

Kenji summarized SEC-2026-014: 37 customers notified, 186k credits, no churn attributed so far, 4 of 5 action items complete, contractor access remediation slipped to Oct 15. Abiodun asked whether we have cyber insurance coverage for credits; answer is partially, 1M retention, so no claim. Board requested a third party assessment of the export pipeline before year end. Budget estimate 85k.

## 5. Rennick

Lena recommended settlement at 2.75M gross. Board agreed. Saarinen asked that the IP strategy (defensive filings, patent pool) come back as a separate item in Q1.

## 6. Project Juniper

Raj presented a 58 role reduction to get burn under 1.2M by end of Q1 2027. Lyle asked about the timing relative to the Ferrolake announcement; Dana prefers announcing Juniper first (Nov 10) and Ferrolake after signing, to avoid the narrative that we are replacing staff with acquired engineers. Abiodun asked for the disparate impact analysis to be shared with the comp committee before Nov 1. Board supports proceeding; formal approval by written consent once final numbers are ready.

## 7. Comp committee

Lyle reported 2027 bands approved Aug 29, merit budget 3.8%. The committee approved a retention pool of 1.4M for 35 people post Juniper. CEO compensation was discussed in executive session without management present (no notes taken).
