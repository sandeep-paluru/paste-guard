Ideal customer profile. Our best enterprise customers have 2,000 to 20,000 employees, an existing cloud data warehouse, a central data team of at least eight people, and a business intelligence tool they are unhappy with. The strongest verticals by win rate are logistics at 41 percent, insurance at 36 percent and healthcare at 29 percent. Avoid public sector deals under 150,000 dollars until FedRAMP is complete.

Discovery questions. Ask how long it takes a business user to get a new metric added to a dashboard. Ask who gets paged when a report is wrong. Ask what percentage of warehouse spend goes to repeated queries. If the answer to the first question is more than two weeks, the deal qualifies for a proof of value.

  * Stage 1, Discover: confirm pain, budget owner, and current tool; exit criteria is a mutual action plan
  * Stage 2, Prove: 21 day proof of value on customer data, maximum two use cases
  * Stage 3, Propose: pricing within discount authority, security review package sent
  * Stage 4, Negotiate: legal redlines, procurement, executive sponsor call
  * Stage 5, Close: signed order form and kickoff date

Handling Varnetta. Varnetta wins on price and on its embedded SDK. Our counter is that Varnetta charges for every connector and its semantic layer requires a dedicated engineer. Use the total cost of ownership calculator, which typically shows Calloway 18 percent cheaper over three years for a 500 seat deployment. Our internal win rate against Varnetta is 47 percent; against Kestrova it is 58 percent.

Handling Kestrova. Kestrova is cheaper but has weak governance. Push row level security and audit logging early in the evaluation. Ask the prospect to request Kestrova's SOC 2 bridge letter, which we understand has a gap of five months.

Pricing tactics. Never lead with discount. Offer the streaming add on free for the first six months instead of reducing seat price, because add on credits do not reset the renewal baseline. End of quarter concessions are permitted only in the final 10 days and must be logged in the deal desk.

Compensation reminders. Multi year deals earn a 1.2x accelerator on year one ACV. Deals with Lantern pre commitments in 2027 earn a 5,000 dollar SPIF. Quota for enterprise AEs in 2027 is 1.45 million dollars of net new ACV.

Security review. Send the security package at Stage 3, not earlier. The package includes the SOC 2 Type II report under NDA, the penetration test summary, and the standard data processing agreement. If the prospect asks about SEC-2026-014, use the approved incident statement only.

SAMPLE DISCOVERY CALL (annotated)
---------------------------------
AE:  How do new metrics get onto a dashboard today?
Buyer: Ticket to the data team, usually two or three weeks.
AE:  What happens in the meantime?
Buyer: People export to Excel and do it themselves.
AE:  So you've got numbers floating around that don't match?
Buyer: Constantly. Finance and ops argued about churn for a month.
      ^^ THIS is the pain. Write down the exact words, use in proposal.

TERRITORY AND ROUTING
---------------------
Enterprise AEs own named accounts above 2,000 employees. Accounts are routed by HQ location: East (ET and CT timezones), West (MT and PT), EMEA (London). Inbound leads above 2,000 employees go to the named AE within 4 business hours or they reroute to the round robin pool. Disputes go to the RVP and are resolved within one week; the rule of thumb is first to logged meeting wins.

Common mistakes we saw in FY26 lost deal reviews: sending pricing before the proof of value (14 deals), single threading through a data engineer with no budget authority (11 deals), and promising Riptrace dates in email (3 deals, one of which ended up in a contract dispute).

If you are unsure about anything in here ask in #sales-enablement or grab Jess Mercado. This playbook gets updated each quarter, check the version at the top.

Escalation. Strategic deals above 500,000 dollars ACV require a deal review with the CRO before Stage 4. Bring the mutual action plan, the champion map and the competitive analysis to the review.
