# Investor Update: Q3 2026

**CONFIDENTIAL - Calloway Metrics**

Version final | Last updated Oct 10, 2026 | Owner: Dana Whitcombe (CEO) | Distribution: Shareholders, board observers | Change note: n/a

CONFIDENTIAL. For Calloway Metrics shareholders and board observers only. Please do not forward. This update contains unaudited financial information and forward looking statements.

Headline numbers. We closed Q3 with 61.7 million dollars of ARR, up 38 percent year over year and 7.9 percent quarter over quarter. Net new ARR for the quarter was 4.5 million dollars, slightly below our plan of 4.9 million dollars, primarily because two large deals slipped into October. Gross margin improved to 76 percent from 73 percent, driven by the migration to reserved compute.

- ARR: 61.7 million dollars (plan 62.1 million)
- Net revenue retention: 112 percent
- Gross revenue retention: 91.2 percent
- Cash and equivalents: 88.4 million dollars
- Monthly net burn: 1.9 million dollars, down from 2.6 million in Q2
- Headcount: 412 full time employees

Wins. We signed Velmont University System and expanded with Harborline Logistics. The AI query assistant reached 22,000 weekly active users across 310 customers. Our enterprise sales cycle shortened from 117 days to 96 days after we introduced the 21 day proof of value.

Challenges. Security incident SEC-2026-014 in July affected 37 customers. We have completed most remediation items and lost no customers as a direct result, though we issued 186,000 dollars in credits. Renewal risk at Cedarpoint Health Network remains our largest concern for Q1. We also settled the Rennick patent litigation for a net cost of 850,000 dollars after insurance.

Strategic update. We have signed a letter of intent to acquire Ferrolake Data Systems, which would accelerate our real time roadmap by more than a year. We expect to close in January 2027. In parallel, we are planning an organizational restructuring in Q4 to bring burn below 1.2 million dollars per month and extend runway past 2029.

Outlook. We are maintaining full year 2026 guidance of 66 million dollars ending ARR. For 2027 the board has approved a plan targeting 92 million dollars of ending ARR and reaching free cash flow breakeven in Q4 2027. We are not planning to raise primary capital in the next 18 months.

Team. We promoted our head of platform to VP of Platform and hired a new VP of Customer Success from a large public software company. Voluntary attrition for the trailing twelve months was 11 percent, below the industry benchmark. Engagement survey participation was 89 percent with an overall favorability of 74 percent, down two points, which we attribute to uncertainty about the macro environment and competitive recruiting from Quillmark. We will share more detail on the organizational changes in the Q4 letter.

Asks. We are hiring ML infrastructure engineers for Project Lantern and would welcome introductions. We are also looking for introductions to CIOs at regional health systems. Thank you for your continued support. The next update will follow the Q4 board meeting.
